🌙 What Is noctis?

noctis is a home for your programming work that stays quiet and out of the way. It is built on Neovim, a well-known text editor that runs inside a plain terminal window, so everything you do happens in one simple, text-based screen instead of a crowd of windows and tabs.

What makes noctis different is how it treats artificial intelligence helpers. Normally, when you ask an AI assistant to change your code, you have to trust that it did the right thing. You look away for a moment, and files have changed. You are not sure what changed, or where, or whether you should keep it.

noctis removes that guesswork. It lets you place an AI coding helper right next to your code, then shows you every single change that helper makes, the moment it happens. If you like a change, you keep it. If you do not, you can undo just that one piece, safely, and leave everything else exactly as it was.

You can run Claude Code, Codex, Opencode or Kimi inside noctis, side by side with your own work. You are always the one in charge.
